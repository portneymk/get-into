Expanded cues from *Mushroom Men* and the film *Pig Hunt*, plus a Eugene Hutz cameo. Quirky, soundtrack-ish, still unmistakably Claypool — the lesser solo record, useful as a connector.

The title is the tell: if "fungi" made you smile, the Wonka Primus album is the deluxe version of that joke.
