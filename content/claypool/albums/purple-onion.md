The studio Frog Brigade record, and the one to play people who "don't like Primus." *Whamola*, *David Makalaster*, *Buzzards of Green Hill* — New Orleans dust on Bay Area weird, with Warren Haynes, Skerik, Mike Dillon, and the Whamola that later haunted a *South Park* theme remix.

From here the map splits cleanly: solo Claypool, live frogs, experimental C2B3, or the Lennon psych records.
