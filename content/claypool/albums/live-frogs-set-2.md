Pink Floyd's *Animals*, start to finish, Frog Brigade style. A novelty on paper and a surprisingly sturdy live album in practice — the cover appetite without irony, which is the Claypool move.

If you liked being walked through someone else's masterpiece, *Lime and Limpid Green* is the studio equivalent. If you want *their* masterpiece in this lane, *Purple Onion*.
