The usual door, and for good reason. *Jerry Was a Race Car Driver*, *Tommy the Cat*, and *Those Damned Blue-Collar Tweekers* are how a lot of people met this whole universe — bass as lead guitar, Herb's odd-time snap, LaLonde's skronk as punctuation.

It is still a Primus record, not a hits compilation. Stay for *Sathington Willoughby* and *Los Bastardos* if the singles already convinced you. Then pick a lane: thicker Primus, earlier Primus, or leave the band name behind.
