Bryan "Brain" Mantia replaces Tim "Herb" Alexander. The bounce changes. *Shake Hands with Beef* and *Over the Falls* sit on a drier, chunkier grid — still Primus, less rubber-band.

People argue about this one. If you like Brain's feel, C2B3 and *Antipop* keep that conversation going. If you don't, the map happily sends you back to *Punchbowl*.
