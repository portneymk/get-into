Tom Morello, Tom Waits, Matt Stone, Fred Durst behind the board on various tracks — the last Primus album before the hiatus, and it sounds like a band trying on other people's jackets. Restless, guest-heavy, a little sour, occasionally great.

If the restlessness is the appeal, C2B3 is the logical non-Primus hop. If you want them to remember they are Primus, skip to the 2003 reunion EP.
