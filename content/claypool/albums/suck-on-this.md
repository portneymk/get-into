A live debut sold out of the back of a car, and it already sounds like Primus: bass in the foreground, jokes that aren't quite jokes, and a room that cannot decide whether to mosh or laugh.

Start here only if you want the club-circuit version — no Interscope polish, no claymation budget. Everyone else can treat it as a side quest after *Frizzle Fry*.
