The third Delirium record: a 14-song concept about morality, AI, and optimization without empathy, with a comic book riding along. *WAP (What a Predicament)* is the door single; the rest is peak-Delirium prog-psych.

If you got here from Primus in three hops, you have used the map correctly. Loop back to *South of Reality* or cross to *Conspiranoid* if you want the same decade in the other band name.
