Claypool produced, engineered, and played most of this himself at Rancho Relaxo, with cameos from Mark Haggard, Adam Gates, Jay Lane, Charlie Hunter, and Henry Rollins. It is the first time the universe clearly isn't Primus — same voice, smaller rooms, more diary than cartoon.

If you liked the intimacy, *Of Whales and Woe* is the grown-up sequel. If you wanted the swamp with more people onstage, the Frog Brigade is waiting.
