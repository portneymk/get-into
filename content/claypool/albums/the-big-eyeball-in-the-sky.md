C2B3: Claypool, Buckethead, Bernie Worrell, Brain. Live they famously did not rehearse. In the studio they wrote actual songs — Funkadelic sci-fi with a sitar cameo from Gabby La La.

If Primus ever felt too verse-chorus, this is the experimental lane at full volume. If it feels like too many geniuses in a room, *Of Whales and Woe* is the palate cleanser.
