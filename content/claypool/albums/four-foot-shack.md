Claypool and Bryan Kehoe, two guys, a lot of twang, originals and covers (including Primus tunes in overalls). Optional on purpose — and surprisingly clarifying. You can hear the songs when the stack of amps is gone.

Use it as a reset, then jump back to whichever electric lane you actually came for.
