Claypool called the Frog Brigade his mid-life crisis band. Live at the Great American Music Hall: Huth, Eenor, Skerik, Jay Lane, Jeff Chimenti — jam-band Claypool with cover-band courage and originals that already sound like *Purple Onion*.

If you came from Primus and this feels like a relief, stay in the frogs. If you wanted a tighter supergroup, 2001 also produced Oysterhead.
