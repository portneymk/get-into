A short covers EP — XTC, The Residents, The Pigface orbit — that doubles as a taste map. This is what Claypool and company loved when they weren't writing fisherman songs.

Useful as a palate cleanser, not a first Primus record. If the cover appetite is the thing you liked, there are two later EPs that scratch it harder.
