For a lot of lifers this is the most *Primus* Primus record: *Wynona's Big Brown Beaver*, *Southbound Pachyderm*, *Professor Nutbutter's House of Treats*. Story-songs, claymation energy, ridiculous virtuosity that still leaves room for tunes.

If the narratives are why you stayed, jump to *The Desaturating Seven* or out to Holy Mackerel. If you want the rhythm section to get uglier, *Brown Album* is the fork.
