The first album solely credited to Les Claypool. *Robot Chicken* is the novelty; *One Better* and *Phantom Patriot* are the point. Skerik, Mike Dillon, Gabby La La — the Fancy Band in studio form.

This is the swamp-psych lane's home record. From here you can stay solo, put overalls on, or finally meet Sean Lennon.
