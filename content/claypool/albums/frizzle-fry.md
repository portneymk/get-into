The alt door. *John the Fisherman*, *Too Many Puppies*, and the title track are the whole thesis: funk that learned thrash, and a narrator who sounds like a dockworker telling campfire stories.

If *Seas of Cheese* felt a little glossy, this is the same band with the dirt left on. If this already feels like home, you can skip forward to *Pork Soda* or sideways into Sausage.
