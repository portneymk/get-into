*My Name Is Mud*, *Mr. Krinkle*, *DMV* — the platinum-weird record, and the one that made Lollapalooza audiences throw actual mud. The low end is thicker than *Cheese*, the jokes bleaker, the bass still the lead singer.

If this is your favorite Primus texture, *Brown Album* leans even drier. If you liked the characters more than the sludge, *Tales from the Punchbowl* is the next storybook.
