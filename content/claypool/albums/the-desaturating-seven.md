Rainbow-eating goblins, after a picture book by Ul de Rico. Late Primus at its most focused: short, colorful, concept-complete, Herb back behind the kit.

If you want more story-albums that aren't Primus, *South of Reality* and the 2026 Delirium record are the hop. If you want them talking about the present tense, *Conspiranoid*.
