The hiatus ends with a five-song EP and a DVD. Herb is back. The new songs are tight, a little rusty in a good way, and the tour that followed played *Seas of Cheese* as a second set.

Treat it as a bridge, not a destination: either return to the 90s catalog they were celebrating, or jump to the 2010s albums.
